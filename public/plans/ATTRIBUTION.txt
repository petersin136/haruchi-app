Bible reading plan attributions
================================

mcheyne.json
------------
Schedule: Robert Murray M'Cheyne's classic daily Bible reading calendar
  (four readings per day: family × 2 + secret × 2).

The calendar content is public-domain historical material. The editable
calendar data used as the conversion source is published by Ben Edgington
under Creative Commons CC0 (waived 2023):

  http://edginet.org/mcheyne/printables.html

Machine-readable day list was converted into Haruchi's JSON shape
({ book, chapters[] } with ASCII book ids matching app/bible-reading/books.ts).
No copyright is claimed on the resulting schedule file.

year365.json / days90.json / days30.json
----------------------------------------
Generated by scripts/build-plans.mjs from Haruchi's own book/chapter counts
(app/bible-reading/books.ts). Even chapter splits across N days; book
boundaries may cross within a day. Original to this project.
